Our story

Built by people with deep co-lending experience.

Mannjal's founders ran partnership lending from the inside and built the infrastructure they wished existed. Credit decisions remain with the regulated lender or agreed participant; Mannjal runs inside each partner's own environment and moves the work between them.

The name

What "Mannjal" stands for.

Mannjal means Turmeric.

Turmeric is a catalyst for good. It strengthens what it touches and adds value without ever needing to be the main ingredient. That is the role we set out to play. We sit between a lender and an originator, make the partnership work, and leave the credit decision and the borrower relationship where they belong.

What we are trying to catalyse is good lending. Productive credit that reaches the businesses and households that can put it to work. The name keeps us close to that, and close to home.
