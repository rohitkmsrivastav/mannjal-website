AI-assisted case operations

One workbench across the case lifecycle

Authorized teams can prepare and move the work through configured stages without losing the evidence, ownership or approvals behind each action.

Create or locate the case

Find an existing application or create a permitted one, select the programme, assign the maker, and confirm the active stage.

Capture people and evidence

Prepare borrower, co-applicant, ownership, branch, loan and KYC or KYB information from supported evidence.

Coordinate credit checks

Bring income, obligations, bank statements, bureau, deduplication, FOIR or DIR, product fit and loan amount checks into view.

Review property evidence

Compare legal and technical reports, ownership, title, CERSAI, valuation and LTV information while preserving unresolved conflicts.

Clear sanction and disbursal gates

Keep maker preparation separate from approval and hold the case when a mandatory condition or document remains incomplete.

Track servicing and closure

Follow post-disbursal documents, tranche restrictions, servicing exceptions, settlement and closure evidence.

Evidence, checks and gates

Every finding carries its context

Supported facts stay tied to their source, checks explain their outcome, and workflow actions remain subject to the programme's roles and approval gates.

01
Evidence

Keep the source with the fact

Record the source filename, page or section and report date. Missing, partial, original, wrong-property or unsupported evidence stays visibly unresolved.

02
Checks

Explain every result

Report configured checks as Pass, Fail or Incomplete with the reason, policy reference, missing evidence and required next action.

03
Gates

Move only permitted work

Prepare queries, deviations or stage actions for review, and move the case only when the configured permissions, approvals and mandatory gates are satisfied.

Human control and auditability

AI prepares. Authorized users approve. The lender decides.

AI can prepare supported facts, checks, queries, deviations, permitted workflow actions and reports. It cannot bypass role permissions, replace human judgement, prove fraud, or independently make or approve a credit decision.

✓No invented facts ✓No silent overwrites ✓A passing check is not approval ✓Stages move only when gates clear ✓Indicators are not proof ✓Actions stay in the audit trail

AI processing is configured within the deployment and data-residency boundaries agreed for each program. Application assistance stays scoped to the relevant case; portfolio answers stay within the logged-in organization's authorized data.
