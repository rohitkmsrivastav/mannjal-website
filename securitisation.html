01
Data from origination

Required fields captured at the point of lending

Loan-level fields needed for configured pass-through certificate workflows are captured when the loan originates. The data is structured from origination.

02
Pool construction

Slice, filter, and package

Build pools by product, geography, vintage or credit quality. Pool tapes can be generated from structured loan data with field-level provenance.

03
Audit trail

Documentation built for investor review

Configured data points can be traced back to the origination event. Timestamped, attributed and exportable evidence supports investor and rating-agency review.

Inside the platform

Certificate-readiness, built into the workflow

The platform, Mannjal's workflow layer, slices pools from structured loan data, timestamps configured changes, and keeps documentation and provenance attached to relevant fields, so the audit pack is easier to assemble.

Why this matters

Securitisation should be a capital decision. The data should not be the bottleneck

When data is structured from origination, your treasury team can decide when to securitise based on market conditions and capital needs, with documentation and audit evidence already organized.
