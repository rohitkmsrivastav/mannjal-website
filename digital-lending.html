Compliance controls

The controls the Digital Lending Guidelines call for, built in from the start.

01
Key Fact Statement generation

Key Fact Statement per loan

Key Fact Statements can be generated for configured loan products, with annual percentage rate, fees and terms formatted to the lender's template and aligned to disclosure requirements.

02
Guarantee tracking

First Loss Default Guarantee, tracked and reconciled

First Loss Default Guarantee commitments are tracked at the program level, with utilisation and expiry visible through the workflow. When caps or reporting requirements change, the configuration updates in one place.

03
Audit trails

Configured actions logged and attributable

Timestamped, role-attributed logs cover configured stages of the lending workflow. They are exportable on demand for internal audit, RBI reporting or partner-level review.

Inside the platform

Borrower checks, routed through one workflow.

The platform routes borrower checks through the providers you already contract, and logs the result against the same case file that supports the Key Fact Statement and audit trail.
