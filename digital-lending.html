Compliance controls

The controls the DLG calls for, built in from the start.

01
KFS generation

Key Fact Statement per loan

KFS generated automatically for every loan, with the APR, fees, and terms the borrower needs to see. Formatted to the lender's template, compliant with the DLG disclosure requirements.

02
FLDG handling

First Loss Default Guarantee, tracked and reconciled

FLDG commitments tracked at the program level, with utilisation and expiry visible in real time. When the DLG caps or reporting requirements change, the configuration updates in one place.

03
Audit trails

Every action logged and attributable

Timestamped, role-attributed logs for every stage of the lending workflow. Exportable on demand for internal audit, RBI reporting, or partner-level review.
