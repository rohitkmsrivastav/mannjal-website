The work

Built from scratch, close to the problem.

Everything at Mannjal is built in-house: the workflow engines, the reconciliation, the models, and the co-pilots that sit on top of them. This is product work at the intersection of AI and lending, where the systems have to hold up inside regulated financial operations.

People here learn the technology deeply and use it to widen what the infrastructure can do on its own. The surface area is broad, the problems are new, and the work is real.

The people

A team that goes and finds the answer.

Mannjal attracts and keeps people who are curious, driven, and enterprising, the kind who take a problem further than they were asked to and build the tools they need along the way. It stays a small group, and the bar for joining it is high.
