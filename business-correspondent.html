How it works

From agent onboarding to field-level audit.

01
Agent onboarding

KYC and geo-verification for configured agents

New agents go through identity verification, geo-tagging and document collection before activation. The lender sets the criteria; Mannjal applies those checks across the configured network.

02
Field-to-digital journeys

Field capture that meets digital standards

Borrower data is captured in the field via mobile, with document checklists applied at the point of collection. Files can reach the lender's desk with fewer avoidable gaps.

03
Monitoring

Cash-flow limits and SLA tracking

Cash-flow limits are monitored per agent. Capture SLAs are tracked through the workflow, so the ops team can see bottlenecks without chasing agents over the phone.

Inside the platform

The field, tracked case by case.

The platform, Mannjal's workflow layer, carries configured agents and borrower files from onboarding to audit evidence, so the ops team can see the network without chasing agents over the phone.
