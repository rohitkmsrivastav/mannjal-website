What Mannjal handles

From originator onboarding to field-level audit

01
Originator onboarding

Due diligence and geo-verification for configured originators

Originators go through the lender's defined due-diligence, geo-verification and document requirements before activation. The lender sets the criteria; Mannjal applies those checks across the configured network.

02
Field-to-digital journeys

Field capture that meets digital standards

Borrower data is captured in the field via mobile, with document checklists applied at the point of collection. Files can reach the lender's desk with fewer avoidable gaps.

03
Monitoring

Cash-flow limits and SLA tracking

Cash-transaction limits are monitored per originator. Capture SLAs are tracked through the workflow so the ops team can see bottlenecks directly, without manual follow-up.

Inside the platform

The field, tracked case by case

The platform, Mannjal's workflow layer, carries configured originators and borrower files from onboarding to audit evidence, giving the ops team a single view of the network.
