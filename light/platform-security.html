Platform & security

Runs in your environment. We don't hold your data.

Mannjal deploys as a dedicated instance inside your own cloud or data centre, with role-based access and maker-checker controls.

What your IT committee will check

Four things, answered plainly.

01
Deployment

Your cloud, your tenancy

Each engagement runs as its own instance inside your AWS account or on-premise environment. No shared tenancy.

02
Data isolation

Stays in your environment

Mannjal runs inside your own instance. We don't hold your data centrally; it stays in your environment, and access is role-based with maker-checker controls.

03
Credentials & access

Your keys stay yours

Every integration uses your own API credentials with the providers you already contract. Mannjal routes and logs the calls; it does not proxy your keys.

04
Audit logs

One export, not a search

Every action, API call, and document event is timestamped, attributed, immutable, and exportable on demand. An audit of any single loan is one export.
