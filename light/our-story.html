Our story

Built by the people who built India's co-lending playbook.

Mannjal's founders ran partnership lending from the inside and saw exactly where it broke. The lender always makes the credit decision; Mannjal runs inside each partner's own environment and moves the work between them.

The short version

Where we are, and how we got here.

2023–24

Founded in Bengaluru and built the product alongside live lending partners, moving from manual operations to a working system.

2025

Crossed ₹1,000 Cr in disbursements across a network of originators and lenders.

Dec 2025
