How it works

Two sides, one clean workflow.

Each partner works in their own instance. Mannjal moves the file between them, runs the checks in the middle, and routes the decision back to the lender. It never lends and never competes with either side.

What we run

One operating layer for every lending partnership.

Five partnership structures, one operating layer.

Co-lending

One blended rate, your risk split, reconciliation handled for you. Adding the next co-lender is a config change, not a project.

Business correspondent

Agent onboarding, KYC and document checklists, and phygital journeys, so your BC network grows without growing the ops desk.

DA

Loan-level data kept structured from origination, so a pool tape or investor cut is a click, not a fire drill.

Securitization

PTC-ready data from day one, so a securitization is a button, not a quarter of rebuilding.

Digital lending

RBI Digital Lending-aligned programs (KFS, FLDG, audit trails), so you launch compliant and stay that way.
