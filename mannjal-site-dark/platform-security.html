What your IT committee will check

Four things, answered plainly.

01
Deployment

Your cloud, your tenancy

Deploy Mannjal inside your own infrastructure, your cloud server or your physical servers, as a dedicated instance with no shared tenancy. Processing happens in your environment and data never leaves your perimeter.

02
Data isolation

Stays in your environment

Mannjal runs inside your own instance. We don't hold your data centrally; it stays in your environment, which supports your data-localization and DPDP obligations, with role-based access and maker-checker controls.

03
Credentials & access

Your keys stay yours

Every integration uses your own API credentials with the providers you already contract. Mannjal routes and logs the calls; it does not proxy your keys.

04
Audit logs

A full audit in one export

Every action, API call, and document event is timestamped, attributed, immutable, and exportable on demand. An audit of any single loan is one export.

The evidence

Independently verified.

The Mannjal platform stores and processes data in India only, Mumbai primary with Delhi as DR, and blocks cross-border transfer at the platform layer. It is independently audited by a CERT-In empanelled assessor against the RBI data-localisation framework, with no exceptions noted.

✓AES-256 at rest ✓TLS 1.3 in transit ✓WAF, OWASP-hardened ✓IP-whitelisting ✓2FA ✓Role-based access

Each control was checked during the independent audit. Built and operated in alignment with ISO 27001:2022. Residency and audit statements describe the Mannjal platform; the production scope of a specific deployment may differ.
