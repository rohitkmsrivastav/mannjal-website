Why we stay in the middle

A neutral party, by design

Mannjal never makes a credit decision and never holds borrower data. Each lender and originator runs their own instance, and the firm's only role is to move work between them cleanly. That neutrality is what lets two parties who would otherwise not share a system work on the same one.

Closes on the one principle a board sponsor needs to trust before a bank commits: Mannjal isn't a competitor to either side. Mirrors the reassurance bands on For Banks and Platform & Security — same role on the diligence page.
