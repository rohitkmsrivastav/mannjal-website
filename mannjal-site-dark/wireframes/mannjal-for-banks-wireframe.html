Three ways banks run partnerships on Mannjal

One paragraph each. No feature bullets, no "platform" language. Bank's words, not SKUs.

BC lending

Banks are signing up BC partners right now. The work is in standing each one up: eligibility logic, documentation formats, reconciliation, audit trails. Mannjal sets this up once and clones it for every new partner, so your team handles the tenth program at the same effort as the first.

Co-lending

Across multiple co-lending partners, files come in different formats from different systems. Your team burns time on coordination instead of credit. Mannjal standardizes intake, runs eligibility automatically, generates the RBI documentation, and keeps the audit trail clean. Your team reviews exceptions, not every file.

DA and securitization

Pool tapes, tranche documents, and investor data requests usually mean pulling from several systems. Mannjal keeps loan-level data structured from origination, so reports export on demand instead of getting rebuilt each time.
