Positioning guardrail

Dash runs the process. The lender owns the credit decision.

Dash mirrors the operating process agreed between participating entities. Workflows, stages, tasks, business rules, user roles, maker-checker controls, document requirements, dashboards, reports, integrations, and exports are configured for the program.

Mannjal provides the technology, workflow configuration, integrations layer, and platform operations. Credit policy, underwriting, approvals, borrower relationship ownership, lending funds, and final lending outcomes remain with the regulated lender or the relevant contracted program participant, as applicable. Dash executes configured workflows and records process evidence; it is not a credit decision engine.

Replace scattered handoffs with one operating trail.

Partnership lending programs often run across emails, spreadsheets, manual follow-ups, and disconnected document movement. Dash gives teams one structured workflow for what was submitted, what was validated, what was flagged, who acted on it, what changed, and what remains pending.

Core workflow

From application creation to lender handoff.

Dash gives each program a controlled workflow for moving an application from intake to review, query resolution, approval handoff, reporting, and export.

01

Create or ingest the application

Partners and field teams create loan applications through structured data entry, document upload, and configured Excel or CAM ingestion.

02

Validate data and document readiness

Dash checks mandatory data, required documents, format errors, eligibility conditions, and configured policy or readiness rules.

03

Route tasks, queries, and exceptions

Teams assign queries, track task ownership, manage dependencies, close clarifications, and move cases through program-specific routes.

04

Review with role-based controls

Users see, update, review, or approve only the parts of the case permitted by their assigned role, section visibility, and maker-checker steps.

05

Report, generate, and export

Dashboards, MIS, scheduled reports, reminders, Word and Excel generation, and configured exports support downstream processing and audit review.
