Build the infrastructure that partnership lending runs on.

Mannjal is a small, focused team in Bengaluru. We're seed-funded by B Capital and Arali Ventures, live in production with banks and originators, and growing.

What we're building

One infrastructure for every lending partnership in India.

Co-lending, Business Correspondent, Direct Assignment, securitisation, and digital lending, across MSME, agri, housing finance and supply chain. Each partner works in their own secure instance; the infrastructure moves the work between them. Over 70 originators and 20 senior lenders already run on it.

Where we're hiring

Three areas, one product.

We're looking for people who want to work close to a hard problem. Roles are in Bengaluru.

01
Engineering

Backend, infra and data

Multi-tenant architecture, workflow engines, escrow reconciliation, and real-time MIS for regulated lending. You build systems financial institutions rely on daily.

02
Product

Lending workflows and lender experience

You define how a co-lending program gets configured, how a Business Correspondent agent captures a file in the field, and how a lender's credit team reviews it. The users are ops managers at banks and NBFCs.

03
Go-to-market

Lender relationships and partnerships

You talk to banks, SFBs and NBFCs about their partnership lending operations and help them see what changes when the infrastructure is right. The sale is a walkthrough of the live product.
