What Mannjal handles

From program setup to partner-level reporting

01
Program setup

Configure agreed program rules across partners

We configure your defined eligibility rules, exposure split, blended rate, product parameters, approval flow and compliance thresholds. Each partner setup can then be adapted to its agreed requirements without rebuilding the workflow from scratch.

02
File orchestration

Checks, gaps and queries routed upfront

Eligibility, policy, document, bureau and KYC checks route through the lenders' approved providers. Gaps, deviations and queries are surfaced before the file reaches the credit desk.

03
Reconciliation

Escrow, reporting and RBI returns from structured data

Disbursement and recovery activity is reconciled across partnerships. Partner-level management reports are generated from the same structured loan-level data that supports escrow routing.

Inside the platform

The partnership configuration defines the agreed checks, roles and reporting. Each co-lending case then moves through its own intake, review, sanction and reconciliation workflow.
